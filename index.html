How to play

Four players share one queen. Each of you owns a castle only you can see. Get the queen to land on yours and you win instantly.

Every round

Drop coins on the cells around the queen. Everyone does this in secret, at the same time. Then the coins are counted up by direction.

Opposite sides cancel

Up 3 against Down 2 leaves Up 1.
Right 2 is unopposed.
Right is stronger, so the queen slides 2 cells right.

Equal strength on both axes means she stays put — and the coins are spent anyway. Betting nothing is always allowed.

Landing is everything

She must finish her slide on your castle. Gliding over it does nothing. Same for the treasure stacks.

Treasure

You always have one secret treasure stack only you can see. Land on it to bank the coins. It shrinks by one for every cell the queen travels, so the longer you take, the less it is worth.

Coins

Coins are gone whether they win or cancel. Everyone is refilled only when all four players are empty.

What stays secret

Other castles, other treasure, other balances, and who bet what. You are told which way the queen went, never who pushed her.

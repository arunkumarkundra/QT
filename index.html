How to play

Four players. One queen everybody shares. Each of you owns a castle that only you can see. Move the queen onto your castle and you win instantly.

Nobody drives the queen

Every round you secretly place coins on the four cells around her. When all four players lock in, the stakes are added up by direction.

Opposite stakes cancel

Up 3, Down 2, Right 2, Left 0.
Up and Down cancel down to Up 1. Right still has 2.
Right is the strongest survivor, so the queen moves 2 cells right.

If two directions survive with equal force, the queen does not move — and every coin is still spent. Spending nothing is always a legal choice.

Distance is a maximum, not a promise

A surviving force of 7 moves the queen up to seven cells. If the wall arrives first she stops there and the remaining steps are thrown away.

Landing is everything

The queen must finish her move on your castle. Sailing straight over it does nothing. The same is true of your private bonus challenge.

Your private bonus

You always have one secret bonus cell worth 10, 20 or 30 coins. Land the queen on it to bank the reward. Every cell the queen travels knocks one coin off the reward, so a distant bonus can rot away to nothing before you ever reach it. When it is claimed or hits zero, a fresh one appears somewhere else.

Coins

You start with 75. Coins are spent whether they win, cancel or achieve nothing at all. Everyone is topped up only when all four players have run dry.

What you never see

Other castles. Other bonuses. Other balances. Who staked what. The queen's history. After each round you see only the combined totals per direction — never who paid for them. Everything is revealed when someone wins.

Controls

  • Click a highlighted cell or an arrow to add coins.
  • Shift-click or right-click to take coins back.
  • Arrow keys stake, Enter locks, Backspace clears.
  • Keys 1, 2 and 3 switch between staking 1, 5 and 10 at a time.
